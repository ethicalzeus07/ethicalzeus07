## 👋 About

MS Computer Science student at **New York University** (graduating May 2028), with 2.5 years of engineering and production-support experience at Arizona State University.

I build **LLM and agent systems end to end**: retrieval pipelines that can show their sources, agents that hold state across multi-step tasks, and the Python and TypeScript services behind them. The thread through all of it is people who usually get designed around. My main research project was a voice copilot that let blind and low-vision students operate 3D STEM simulations, and the version that shipped looked nothing like my first design, because testing with those students said so.

---

## 🚀 What I'm working on

- **Agentic systems and RAG** — retrieval that traces every answer back to its source, agents with real state handling rather than one-shot prompts
- **Cloud backends** — FastAPI and Next.js services on AWS Lambda, Cloud Run, and Kubernetes, provisioned with Terraform
- **Evaluation** — building out eval sets for retrieval quality, because "it looked right" is not a measurement
- 🏆 **Supermemory Track Winner, VillageHacks 2025** · YC Hack the Stackathon participant
